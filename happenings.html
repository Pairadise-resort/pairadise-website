This month at Pairadise

Happenings

Pairadise is more than a place to sleep, it's a place to gather. Here's what's on this month, from Sunday brunch to full moon nights, plus a look at what we're building next.

Every Sunday

Vegan Brunch & Music Jam

Join us for a relaxed community morning: a vegan buffet, good company, and an open music jam. Come as a guest, come as a listener, or come with an instrument and join the circle.

When

Every Sunday, 11:11 AM – 3:15 PM

What

Vegan buffet lunch with a live, open music jam

Price

99 THB per person

Bring an instrument

Play with us and your lunch is on the house

Every lunch ticket also enters you into our lucky draw, with a chance to win small prizes or a discount on your next lunch with us. All are welcome, whether you're staying at Pairadise or just visiting for the day.

Every full moon, after dark

Full Moon Gathering

We gather every full moon for an evening around the fire, with an open music jam and a bonfire that runs late into the night. It's an easy, unplugged night of shared songs, good conversation, and community.

This month, we're hosting a family visiting from China, whose musically-inspired kids joined right in on a live jam session. It's exactly the kind of spontaneous, cross-cultural moment this gathering is built for, come see who shows up next.

Under construction

What We're Building

We're currently building a new dance hall and workshop space: a geodesic dome designed to host movement classes, workshops, and gatherings of its own. We're excited to get it finished and open it up to guests.

Alongside it, we're working on a second structure built from natural materials, inspired by the weaver birds who've made their own community right here at the resort. Their woven, communal nests are the starting point for the design.

We're excited to share our progress as it happens. If you're curious about either build, just ask us, we're happy to set up a VR simulation so you can walk through the construction before it's finished.

Where you'll be

Quiet, but close to everything

Pairadise sits in Baan Mae Yen, just outside the heart of Pai. You're tucked into gardens, mountain views, and natural water, yet only a 10-15 minute walk or a short scooter ride to Pai walking street, cafes, and night market.

It's the best of both: peaceful sleep at night, and the buzz of town whenever you want it.
