Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

What this page covers

This page explains what data pairadise-resort.github.io collects, what cookies this website uses, and how you can control them. It's written in plain language rather than dense legal text, but it's meant to reflect how the site actually works.

Cookies we use

We keep this to a minimum. Here's everything that can set a cookie or store data in your browser when you visit:

What Purpose Category
Cookie consent choice Remembers whether you accepted or declined non-essential cookies, so we don't ask you every visit. Necessary
YouTube embedded video The video on our homepage is hosted by YouTube (Google). Loading it may set YouTube/Google cookies, whether or not you press play. Functional / third-party
Google Fonts We load some typefaces from Google Fonts. This does not set cookies, but your browser does make a request to Google's servers to fetch them, which exposes your IP address to Google in the same way any external resource would. Functional / third-party

We do not currently use analytics (like Google Analytics), advertising cookies, or tracking pixels. If that changes, we'll update this table and, where required, ask for your consent before anything loads.

Your choices

When you first visit, a banner lets you choose Accept All or Necessary Only. You can change your mind at any time using the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of any page.

You can also block or delete cookies directly through your browser's settings. Blocking necessary cookies may affect how parts of the site work, such as remembering your cookie preference itself.

Other data we collect

If you fill out our "Register Your Interest" form, we collect your name, email, and whatever else you choose to share, so we can contact you about retreats and gatherings. We don't sell or share this information with third parties for marketing purposes.

If you message us directly (WhatsApp, phone, or email), we use that information only to respond to you and manage your booking or enquiry.

Contact us about your data

For any questions, or to request a copy or deletion of your data, reach us at management.pairadise@gmail.com or via the contact details in the footer below.

This policy is intended as a clear, good-faith summary of our data practices, not a substitute for legal advice. Depending on where our guests are visiting from, additional region-specific disclosures (for example under EU/UK GDPR or Thailand's PDPA) may apply, and we'd recommend a professional legal review before relying on this page as a complete compliance document.
